About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Mechanical Engineering at the University of New Hampshire specializing in computational mechanics and computational materials science.

My research focuses on developing computational frameworks that connect material microstructure with macroscopic mechanical behavior.

My work combines finite element analysis, crystal plasticity, image-based modeling, computational homogenization, molecular dynamics, density functional theory, and machine learning.

My broader research interests include structural metals, porous materials, biomaterials, nanomaterials, multifunctional materials, and energy-related systems.

Research Architecture

From Atoms to Engineering Response

My research spans multiple length scales, linking atomic interactions and microstructural features with continuum-scale deformation and effective engineering properties.

01 · ATOMIC

Atomistic Modeling

Density functional theory and molecular dynamics for nanostructures, interfaces, functional materials, and energy-storage systems.

02 · MICRO

Microstructure

μCT, EBSD, segmentation, image processing, microstructure reconstruction, and representative volume elements.

03 · CRYSTAL

Crystal Plasticity

CPFEM modeling of crystallographic slip, anisotropy, localized plasticity, grain-scale response, and deformation mechanisms.

04 · CONTINUUM

Finite Element Modeling

Constitutive modeling and nonlinear finite element analysis of advanced engineering materials.

05 · MACRO

Homogenization

Prediction of effective mechanical properties of heterogeneous and porous materials using RVEs.

Crystal Plasticity & Computational Mechanics

Development and validation of crystal plasticity finite element models to investigate deformation mechanisms, anisotropic plasticity, slip-system activity, grain-boundary effects, and local mechanical response.

Image-Based Finite Element Modeling

Development of automated computational workflows for converting three-dimensional μCT images into simulation-ready representative volume elements.

Multiscale Materials Modeling

Integration of continuum mechanics, finite element simulation, crystal plasticity, and atomistic modeling to connect microscopic mechanisms with macroscopic behavior.

Computational Materials Science

Application of DFT, molecular dynamics, machine learning, and scientific computing to advanced structural, nanostructured, and energy-related materials.
